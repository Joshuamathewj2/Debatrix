# Renewable Energy Transition
IRENA reports that deployment of renewable electricity technologies has expanded across global power systems.
https://www.irena.org/Publications/2024/Sep/Renewable-Power-Generation-Costs-in-2023

IRENA's cost report documents substantial reductions in the cost of solar and wind power over the 2010–2023 period.
https://www.irena.org/Publications/2024/Sep/Renewable-Power-Generation-Costs-in-2023

Integrating variable wind and solar generation requires attention to transmission, storage, and flexibility in the wider power system.
https://www.iea.org/reports/integrating-solar-and-wind

The pace and cost of the energy transition depend on local resource quality, grid readiness, financing, and policy choices.
https://www.iea.org/reports/integrating-solar-and-wind
