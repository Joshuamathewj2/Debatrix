# School Uniform Evidence
The Education Endowment Foundation's evidence review finds extremely limited evidence that school uniforms improve attainment.
https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/teaching-learning-toolkit/school-uniform

Uniform policies vary from a required school sweatshirt to more formal rules that specify multiple clothing items.
https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/teaching-learning-toolkit/school-uniform

The limited evidence base makes it difficult to predict the academic effect of a uniform policy in a particular school.
https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/teaching-learning-toolkit/school-uniform

Schools considering uniforms must define the policy in context, including the required items and the practical costs for families.
https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/teaching-learning-toolkit/school-uniform
