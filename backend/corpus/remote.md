# Remote Work Research
A randomized trial at Trip.com tested a hybrid schedule in which employees worked from home two days each week.
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07500-2

The study reported that the hybrid schedule reduced employee attrition by about one-third.
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07500-2

The researchers found no negative effect on performance reviews or promotion outcomes during the study period.
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07500-2

The results come from one company and one hybrid arrangement, so they do not establish effects for every occupation or fully remote work.
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07500-2
