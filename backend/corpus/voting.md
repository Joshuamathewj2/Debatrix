# Voting and Civic Participation
International IDEA's Voter Turnout Database compiles participation statistics from national elections around the world.
https://www.idea.int/data-tools/data/voter-turnout-database

The database distinguishes turnout measures and provides information that allows comparisons across election types and countries.
https://www.idea.int/data-tools/data/voter-turnout-database

Turnout rates vary between countries and elections, so a single global figure can hide important local differences.
https://www.idea.int/data-tools/data/voter-turnout-database

Registration rules, election access, institutional design, and political competition are factors researchers examine when studying participation.
