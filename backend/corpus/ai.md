# Governing Artificial Intelligence
The OECD AI Principles call for AI systems to respect human rights and democratic values, including fairness, privacy, and the rule of law.
https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles

The OECD principles also emphasize transparency, robustness, security, and accountability across the AI system lifecycle.
https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles

The NIST AI Risk Management Framework organizes voluntary risk work into Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage functions.
https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework

NIST describes its framework as voluntary guidance intended to help organizations manage risks to individuals, organizations, and society.
https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
