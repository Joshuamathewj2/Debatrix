# Basic Income Evidence
Finland's 2017–2018 basic income experiment provided a monthly payment to 2,000 unemployed people selected for the study.
https://www.kela.fi/basic-income-experiment

Participants received €560 per month without the payment being reduced when they took work.
https://www.kela.fi/basic-income-experiment

The evaluation reported improved perceived economic security and mental wellbeing among participants.
https://www.kela.fi/basic-income-experiment

The experiment found small employment effects and studied a defined group over a limited period, rather than a permanent nationwide program.
https://www.kela.fi/basic-income-experiment
