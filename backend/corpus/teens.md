# Social Media and Adolescent Wellbeing
The US Surgeon General's advisory describes both potential benefits and risks of social media use for young people.
https://www.hhs.gov/surgeongeneral/priorities/youth-mental-health/social-media/index.html

The advisory says current evidence does not establish that social media is sufficiently safe for children and adolescents.
https://www.hhs.gov/surgeongeneral/priorities/youth-mental-health/social-media/index.html

The National Academies review calls for nuanced research and cautions against treating all social media use or all adolescents as the same.
https://nap.nationalacademies.org/catalog/27396/social-media-and-adolescent-health

Researchers identify measurement, platform differences, and the direction of cause and effect as challenges in studying adolescent social media use.
https://nap.nationalacademies.org/catalog/27396/social-media-and-adolescent-health
