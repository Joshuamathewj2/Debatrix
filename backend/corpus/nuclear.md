# Nuclear Energy and Climate
The IPCC assesses nuclear power as a low lifecycle greenhouse gas electricity source compared with fossil fuel generation.
https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg3/

The International Energy Agency describes nuclear power as a dispatchable low-emissions source that can complement variable renewables.
https://www.iea.org/reports/nuclear-power-in-a-clean-energy-system

Nuclear projects can involve high upfront costs and long construction timelines, which affect how quickly capacity can be added.
https://www.iea.org/reports/nuclear-power-in-a-clean-energy-system

Long-term waste management, safety, financing, and project delivery remain central considerations in nuclear policy.
https://www.iea.org/reports/nuclear-power-in-a-clean-energy-system
